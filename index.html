How it works

Your GPS
is the game

No treadmills, no faking steps, no couch victories. Zombies spawn on the real streets around you and chase at real running speeds.

01 They spawn

Zombies appear around your live position, on your actual streets. Daytime brings more of them, faster. Night brings fewer, but they track you longer.

02 You scavenge

Ammo crates and slow fields drop across your city. Grab bullets to shoot the close ones, slow fields to buy yourself a breath.

03 You outrun

The phone buzzes harder as they close in. Eyes on the road, not the screen. Every meter you run is a meter you survive.

How do I start a run?

Grant location permission, step outside, and press play. Zombies and items spawn on the map around your real position — start moving and they start chasing.

Why does the app need background location?

So a run can keep tracking while your screen is off or locked — the zombies don't stop when your display does. Location is only used while a run is active.

Is there a multiplayer mode?

Co-op is in development — you'll see it marked "coming soon" on the mode select screen. We'll announce it when it ships.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device — deleting the app deletes your stats and settings. No account, no server copy, nothing else to remove.

Privacy Policy

Your data, plainly

Last updated: September 3, 2026

What the app uses

  • Precise location — the core of the game. Your position draws you on the map and lets zombies chase you. Background location is used only while a run is active, so play continues with the screen off.
  • Game stats — distances and scores are stored on your device only.
  • Notifications — local notifications only (run stats on your lock screen). We do not send remote push notifications.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data to anyone
  • Use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
  • Track your location when no run is active
  • Upload your location or stats to a server — they stay on your device

Retention & deletion

Your data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. For any data request, email contact.breakaleg@gmail.com.

Children

Break A Leg !! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
