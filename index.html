How do I start a run?

Grant location permission, step outside, and press play. Zombies and items spawn on the map around your real position — start moving and they start chasing.

Why does the app need background location?

So a run can keep tracking while your screen is off or locked — the zombies don't stop when your display does. Location is only used while a run is active.

How does multiplayer work?

Versus and co-op matches run over the internet: your live position and run stats are shared with the other player in your match for the duration of the session. Nothing is shared after the match ends.

How do I delete my data?

Email us at the address above from the account you play with and we'll remove your multiplayer profile and stored stats within 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Your data, plainly

Last updated: September 3, 2026

What the app uses

  • Precise location — the core of the game. Your position draws you on the map and lets zombies chase you. Background location is used only while a run is active, so play continues with the screen off.
  • Multiplayer session data — in versus and co-op matches, your live position and run stats are shared with the other player in your match via Google Firebase, for the duration of the match.
  • Game stats — distances, scores and match results may be stored to operate multiplayer sessions and your game history.
  • Notifications — local notifications only (run stats on your lock screen). We do not send remote push notifications.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data to anyone
  • Use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
  • Track your location when no run or match is active

Retention & deletion

Multiplayer session data is transient. Stored game stats are kept until you ask us to delete them — email contact.breakaleg@gmail.com and we'll remove them within 30 days.

Children

Break A Leg !! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
